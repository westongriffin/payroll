    First 90 days

    By day 90 every tax account is confirmed in good standing or has a named owner and a date, and the first monthly report is in your hands.

    Days 1 to 30

    Verify

    • Pull IRS account transcripts and each state's account status.
    • Get the remediation team's findings and confirm every item is closed.
    • Match tax accounts to where people actually work.
    • Confirm the deposit schedule and whether payroll runs cross the $100,000 next-day line.
    Done whenYou have a written status for every federal, state, local and foreign account.
    Days 31 to 60

    Reconcile

    • Tie the payroll register to deposits, filed returns and the general ledger for the year to date.
    • Audit regular-rate and classification setup.
    • Review whether internal and field staff should stay in one run or move to separate pay groups.
    Done whenEvery variance is explained or corrected, with amended returns filed where needed.
    Days 61 to 90

    Install

    • Compliance calendar live, loaded from the verified account register.
    • Second-person review on every run.
    • Notice log in use.
    • Outside quarterly review scheduled.
    Done whenThe first Payroll Health report is in your hands.

    Depending on start date, year-end falls inside this window: fourth-quarter returns, Form 940 and W-2s, including the first required code TT overtime reporting. That is the first test of these controls, and an outside reviewer checks it before filing.
